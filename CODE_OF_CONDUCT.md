# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and inclusive
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it via
private communication through GitHub (maintainer contact via repository owner).
